About the Company
ABOUT THE COMPANY

A production company built around storytelling

Etai Geva Productions is an independent creative production company working across film, television, stage, music and emerging forms of visual storytelling.

The company develops and produces original content, combining directing, screenwriting, editing, production and visual development — spanning traditional production and new technologies, including AI-assisted filmmaking and video creation.

Ongoing creative research into international developments in cinema, television, theatre, music and production technology feeds directly back into the company's work, turning ideas and visual concepts into compelling productions.
